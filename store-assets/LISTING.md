## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
OCTA is a drum machine that fits in your pocket. Every sound is synthesised
live — there are no samples, no downloads, and no account. Open it and it
makes noise.

EIGHT VOICES
Kick, snare, closed and open hats, clap, tom, rimshot and crash — generated in
real time with pure synthesis. Each one has its own level in the mixer, so you
can sit the hats back and let the kick lead.

A SEQUENCER THAT STAYS IN TIME
The 16-step grid runs on a lookahead scheduler locked to the audio hardware
clock, not a browser timer. That means no drift, even after minutes of
playback, and no stutter when your phone is busy doing something else.

BUILT FOR FINGERS
Pads trigger the instant you touch them. Tempo runs 60–200 BPM and swing goes
0–60%, both adjustable while the pattern is playing — no stopping to tweak.
Tap tempo is there when you want to match something by feel.

FOUR PATTERNS, CHAINED
Write four patterns (A/B/C/D) and switch between them mid-play; the change
lands cleanly on the next bar. Turn on CHAIN and OCTA cycles them
automatically, so a loop becomes an arrangement. Long-press a letter to drop
it from the chain.

LIVE RECORDING
Arm REC and play the pads while the sequencer runs — your hits are written
into the pattern, quantised to the nearest 16th.

YOURS TO KEEP
Everything autosaves on the device. Export a song to a JSON file to back it up
or share it, and import it back on any device.

NO STRINGS
No ads. No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No runtime permissions. OCTA
makes no network requests and works completely offline, on a plane or
underground.

OCTA is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence.
Source: https://github.com/JoskoLatin/OCTA
```
