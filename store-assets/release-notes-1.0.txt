First release of OCTA.

An 8-voice drum machine that runs entirely on your device:
- Kick, snare, closed/open hats, clap, tom, rimshot and crash
- 16-step sequencer that stays locked in time
- 60-200 BPM and 0-60% swing, adjustable while playing
- 4 pattern slots with CHAIN mode
- Live step recording and a per-voice mixer
- Works fully offline. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
