OCTA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

OCTA is an offline drum machine. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, and it has no servers, accounts, ads, or analytics.

Data storage

OCTA saves your patterns, tempo, and mixer settings locally on your device using browser localStorage (web version) or equivalent on-device storage (Android app). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Network access

OCTA makes no network requests during normal use. The web version is a Progressive Web App and, after the first load, works fully offline via a service worker that caches its own static files.

Permissions

The Android app requests no runtime permissions (no microphone, storage, location, contacts, camera, network, etc.). All sound is synthesised on-device with the Web Audio API.

Third parties

OCTA does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting services.

Children's privacy

Because OCTA collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
